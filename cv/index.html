Professional Summary

I develop optimization, uncertainty-quantification and scientific-ML methods for expensive, stiff physical simulations, using combustion chemical kinetics as a primary proving ground. My PhD produced MOSIAC, an open-source workbench for uncertainty-aware combustion mechanism optimization, alongside hands-on experimental work on pool-flame characterization and downdraft gasification.
